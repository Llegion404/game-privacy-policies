Privacy Policy — Stasis

App: Stasis (dev.paradox.stasis, Android) · Published by Llegion404 · Last updated: 2026-10-04

Stasis is an offline one-finger neon arena shooter where time moves only when you move. This policy explains the limited data handling that occurs when the app's optional, personalized features (ads, purchases) are used.

Summary

  • The game itself works fully offline: no account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress (scores, streaks, settings) is stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences) and is never uploaded by us.
  • The app contains Google AdMob ads and one optional Google Play purchase ("Remove ads"), as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads via the Google AdMob SDK:

  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional — they are only shown when you tap the reward button, and grant an in-game reward: tapping SECOND WIND on the death screen once per run revives your ship in place.
  • Interstitial ads appear only when you leave the game-over / end-of-run screen, at most every third game over and no more often than once every two minutes. They never interrupt play and are suppressed after a watched rewarded ad.

AdMob may use the Google advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) to serve and measure ads, and — where you grant consent in the consent dialog shown inside the app (Google's User Messaging Platform, as required in the EEA/UK) — to personalize them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of personalization there. Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases

The app offers a single one-time purchase, "Remove ads", billed by Google Play. The payment is processed entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The purchase record stays with Google Play on your device/account and is used by the app only to switch off ad displays. Purchases can be restored by reinstalling the app with the same Google account.

Data we collect

None. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters with data upload, no accounts, no server. We do not collect, share, or sell personal information. The only third parties that process data are Google (AdMob advertising and Play Billing), as described above.

Children

The app contains no content directed at children under 13 and requests a non-under-age-of-consent tag in its consent flow; ads personalization follows the platform and consent-dialog settings.
