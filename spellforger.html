Privacy Policy — Spellforger

App: Spellforger (com.studio.spellforger, Android) · Published by Llegion404 · Last updated: 2026-09-19

Spellforger is an offline deckbuilding action game. This policy explains the limited data handling that occurs when the app's optional, personalized features (ads) are used.

Summary

  • The game is fully playable offline: no account, no sign-up, no server of ours.
  • Your save game, unlocks, and settings are stored only on your device (local save files) and are never uploaded by us.
  • The app contains Google AdMob ads and optional in-app purchases via Google Play Billing, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads via the Google AdMob SDK:

  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional — they are only shown when you tap the "watch ad" button (for example to revive or double your run reward).
  • Interstitial ads appear only at run end, are frequency-capped (no more than one every two minutes, never during the first runs of a fresh install), and are fully disabled if you own the "Remove Ads" purchase.

AdMob may use the Google advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) to serve and measure ads, and — where you grant consent in the consent dialog shown inside the app (Google's User Messaging Platform, as required in the EEA/UK) — to personalize them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of personalization there. Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (for example "Remove Ads") are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We receive only the purchase result from Google; we never see or store your payment details. Google's payments privacy policy applies: Google Payments privacy notice

Data we collect

None directly. No accounts, no server we operate, no selling or sharing of personal information. Data handling beyond your device is limited to what Google's AdMob and Play Billing services process as described above.

Children

The app contains no content directed at children under 13 and requests a non-under-age-of-consent tag in its consent flow; ads personalization follows the platform and consent-dialog settings.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
