Privacy Policy — Blast Blocks

App: Blast Blocks (com.paradox.blastblocks, Android) · Published by Llegion404 · Last updated: 2026-09-19

Blast Blocks is an offline block puzzle game. This policy explains the limited data handling that occurs when the app's optional, personalized features (ads) are used.

Summary

  • The game itself works fully offline: no account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your game progress and best scores are stored only on your device (Android SharedPreferences) and are never uploaded by us.
  • The app contains Google AdMob ads, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads via the Google AdMob SDK:

  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional — they are only shown when you tap the "watch ad" button on the game-over screen, and grant an in-game reward (for example continuing your run once).
  • Interstitial ads appear only on the game-over screen, at most every third game over and no more often than once every two minutes.

AdMob may use the Google advertising ID (a resettable device identifier) to serve and measure ads, and — where you grant consent in the consent dialog shown inside the app (Google's User Messaging Platform, as required in the EEA/UK) — to personalize them. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of personalization there. Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Data we collect

None. No analytics SDKs, no crash reporters with data upload, no accounts, no server, no in-app purchases. We do not collect, share, or sell personal information.

Children

The app contains no content directed at children under 13 and requests a non-under-age-of-consent tag in its consent flow; ads personalization follows the platform and consent-dialog settings.
